# Asset provenance

Curated graphics used by the LaTeX deck. Some are third-party marks/illustrations
that were part of the original workshop deck. If you redistribute or publish a
build, verify you have the right to use them, or swap in your own:

- `tum-white.png`, `tum-blue.png` - TUM wordmark (institution trademark). Replace
  with your own institution logo via `\wsLogoLight` / `\wsLogoDark` in `config.tex`.
- `k8s-logo.png` - Kubernetes logo (CNCF trademark; see the CNCF brand guidelines).
- `cncf-logo.png` - Cloud Native Computing Foundation logo (CNCF trademark).
- `break-illustration.jpg` - stock-style illustration; confirm its license.
- `author.jpg` - the presenter's own photo.
- `nginx-1.jpg`, `nginx-2.jpg` - screenshots of the nginx demo page.
